# Member 1 CropFinding contract

## Boundary

`CropFindingAgent` is an Admin-only, stateless knowledge-preparation feature. It supports Sri Lankan crop suggestions, variety suggestions for an existing crop, and source-centric reference discovery. Its output is unverified and can only prefill the existing React Admin forms. It never creates a crop, variety, or verified reference.

`CropPlanningCoordinatorAgent`, `CropPlanningTools`, the persisted crop-planning workflow, Members 2–4, and the Flutter farmer application do not call this feature and are unchanged.

## HTTP operations

The React Admin application calls these ASP.NET endpoints:

- `POST /api/crop-finding/suggest-crops`
- `POST /api/crop-finding/suggest-varieties`
- `POST /api/crop-finding/discover-references`

All three require the Admin role. ASP.NET resolves persisted crop and variety names, adds deterministic duplicate labels, strips prohibited resource/inventory rules, and forwards a typed request to the corresponding internal AI-service route. The AI-service routes require the existing service token.

Discovery output remains in React state. Accepted values populate the existing catalog/reference forms; only submitting those existing forms invokes trusted persistence.

## Source-policy verification

The authoritative machine-readable catalog is `ai-service/source_policy/crop_finding_sources.json`. It was checked against the public institutional sites on 2026-10-01 before the MVP catalog was frozen.

The policy uses exact hosts and explicit path prefixes. It does not trust every `.gov.lk`, `.ac.lk`, or other suffix. Entries on shared university or Department of Agriculture hosts are restricted to the configured agriculture paths. Redirects and final URLs are revalidated against the same catalog.

Stage 1 contains these verified Sri Lankan organizations:

- Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock, Land and Irrigation;
- Department of Agriculture and its Rice, Field Crops, Horticultural Crops, Fruit, Seed/Plant Protection, Seed and Planting Material, NAICC, and extension services;
- Department of Export Agriculture;
- Coconut, Tea, Rubber, and Sugarcane research institutes;
- Sri Lanka Cashew Corporation and Palmyrah Development Board;
- National Institute of Post Harvest Management;
- Sri Lanka Council for Agricultural Research Policy;
- Hector Kobbekaduwa Agrarian Research and Training Institute;
- National Institute of Fundamental Studies and National Science Foundation;
- the agriculture faculties/institutes of Peradeniya, PGIA, Ruhuna, Rajarata, Eastern, Jaffna, Wayamba, Sabaragamuwa, and Uva Wellassa.

Stage 2 contains only these verified international fallback organizations:

- FAO;
- International Rice Research Institute;
- World Vegetable Center;
- ICRISAT;
- CIMMYT;
- ICARDA;
- International Potato Center;
- CABI.

Stage 2 is queried only for a specific evidence gap. Its output is labeled `International fallback` and cannot establish Sri Lankan regional suitability or local-variety facts without independent Sri Lankan evidence.

### Excluded or replaced sources

- `scs.doa.gov.lk` was not retained because its live content was no longer reliably identifiable as the official Seed Certification Service. No unrelated substitute was trusted. Only the explicitly verified Department of Agriculture-hosted `/scppc`, `/scs`, `/pps`, and `/spmdc` paths are configured.
- General government and university suffixes are excluded. A newly discovered Sri Lankan source remains unapproved until a developer reviews and adds an exact catalog entry.

## Evidence and review rules

Evidence states are `Supported`, `Partially Supported`, `Unsupported`, `Conflict`, and `Manual Review Required`. Every evidence item retains its source, original and final URL, source classification, evidence excerpt, and page or section when available.

- Supported values can be accepted into the local form draft.
- Partially Supported values require a separate warning confirmation.
- Conflict values require an explicit source-specific choice or Admin edit.
- Unsupported and Manual Review Required values cannot be populated through AI acceptance.
- A persisted reference has one Admin-selected primary source. Switching primary source restores the pre-AI form snapshot so claims from different sources are not mixed.
- `ResourceRequirement`, fertilizer, irrigation, seed/resource quantity, inventory, feasibility, and reservation rules are prohibited at both AI-service and ASP.NET boundaries.
- `Verified At` is never generated by AI; the Admin supplies it during final verification.

## Separate consistency recommendation

The existing runtime coordinator selects a verified reference using crop, variety, region, and active/version criteria. Member 2 later performs another reference lookup that does not repeat all of those criteria. This can allow the two runtime stages to use different reference profiles.

This is not part of CropFinding and was intentionally not changed. A separate, small integration change should pass the coordinator-selected reference identifier/revision forward or centralize the exact selection function, with Member 2 regression tests and team approval.
